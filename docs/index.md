# Widdershins
Counter big tech with your own homestead on the web.

## The vision: A personal home on the social web
> **Every person deserves a home on the web. Content, relationships, and activities belong to people, not platforms.**

You should have a **personal web environment**.

Widdershins is not just another blogging platform, social network, content management system, or personal cloud. It is an integrated environment that combines elements of all four.

The environment provides five core capabilities:

1. **Personal identity:** A persistent identity for participating in the social web.
2. **Personal publishing:** One place for writing, photographs, videos, music, bookmarks, collections, and other content.
3. **Social relationships:** Friends, followers, groups, conversations, and sharing, all on your own site.
4. **Collection and Curation:** A unified interface for following people and websites.
5. **Shared experiences:** Activities people can enjoy together, such as games, collaborative projects, and community events.

### Four architectural principles

1. **Local ownership, global connectivity** -- Each installation owns its users' content and data. Federation provides connectivity without requiring a central service.
2. **Content-type neutrality** -- A photograph, bookmark, blog post, video, and status update are different representations of a common concept: something a person wants to preserve or share. They shouldn't require separate sites, separate accounts, or separate social graphs.
3. **Social interaction without engagement manipulation** -- Chronological feeds, explicit subscriptions, user-controlled filters, and transparent discovery. No algorithmic feed whose primary objective is maximizing time spent on the platform. Spend as much time as you want here. Then go touch grass.
4. **Progressive adoption** -- A personal space should be useful from day one. Features can be enabled individually, as you need them.
